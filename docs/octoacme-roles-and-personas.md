# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## Scrum Master

### Role Summary
The Scrum Master facilitates agile ceremonies, removes blockers, and coaches the team on continuous improvement. They serve as a shield between the delivery team and external disruptions.

### Responsibilities
- Facilitate sprint planning, daily standups, sprint reviews, and retrospectives
- Identify and remove impediments to team progress
- Coach the team on agile practices and iterative delivery
- Track and report on process improvement actions
- Foster a psychologically safe and high-performing team environment

### Goals
- Keep delivery flow predictable and unblocked
- Continuously improve team processes and velocity
- Reduce friction in cross-functional handoffs

### Typical Interactions
- Works daily with Developers and Project Managers to surface and resolve blockers
- Partners with Product Managers to maintain a healthy, prioritized backlog
- Escalates systemic risks or dependencies to the Project Manager
- Supports the Project Manager during retrospectives and planning ceremonies

---

## UX Designer / Researcher

### Role Summary
UX Designers and Researchers ensure that product solutions are usable, accessible, and valuable to end users. They translate user insights into design specs that guide engineering and product decisions.

### Responsibilities
- Conduct user research, interviews, and usability testing
- Create wireframes, mockups, and interactive prototypes
- Define and document interaction patterns and design standards
- Collaborate with Product Managers to refine requirements and acceptance criteria
- Validate implemented features against design intent before release

### Goals
- Deliver intuitive, accessible experiences that meet user needs
- Reduce rework caused by late discovery of usability issues
- Ensure design consistency across the product

### Typical Interactions
- Collaborates closely with Product Managers from discovery through delivery
- Reviews implementation with Developers during build to verify design fidelity
- Shares user research findings with Business Stakeholders and Product Managers
- Works with the Data Analyst to measure the impact of UX changes post-release

---

## Data Analyst

### Role Summary
Data Analysts provide reporting, analytics, and actionable insights that inform prioritization, measure success metrics, and support evidence-based decision-making across teams.

### Responsibilities
- Define, track, and report on project and product success metrics
- Build and maintain dashboards and analytics pipelines
- Partner with Product Managers to design experiments and interpret results
- Identify trends, anomalies, and opportunities in product or operational data
- Present findings in a clear, accessible format for both technical and non-technical audiences

### Goals
- Enable data-informed prioritization and investment decisions
- Validate that delivered features achieve intended outcomes
- Surface risks and opportunities early through proactive monitoring

### Typical Interactions
- Collaborates with Product Managers to define KPIs and success criteria during planning
- Works with Developers to instrument features and ensure data quality
- Provides UX Designers/Researchers with quantitative data to complement qualitative research
- Reports key metrics and insights to Business Stakeholders at review milestones

---

## Business Stakeholder

### Role Summary
Business Stakeholders provide strategic direction, funding, and high-level feedback. They validate that project outcomes align with organizational priorities and ensure cross-functional visibility.

### Responsibilities
- Define and communicate organizational priorities and constraints
- Approve major deliverables, scope changes, and go/no-go decisions
- Review project status, risks, and budget at key milestones
- Provide domain expertise and sponsor adoption of delivered changes
- Escalate strategic blockers and resolve competing priorities

### Goals
- Ensure projects deliver measurable business value
- Maintain alignment between project outcomes and organizational strategy
- Minimize strategic risk and support successful adoption

### Typical Interactions
- Engages with the Project Manager and Product Manager at milestone reviews and decision points
- Reviews risk and status reports prepared by the Project Manager
- Consults with the Data Analyst on ROI and impact metrics post-release
- Participates in stakeholder briefings and executive updates

---

## Release Manager

### Role Summary
The Release Manager plans, coordinates, and oversees the release process. They act as the central point of coordination across development, QA, operations, and stakeholders to ensure smooth, low-risk deployments.

### Responsibilities
- Plan and schedule release windows in coordination with Development and Operations
- Own the release checklist and ensure all pre-release gates are met
- Coordinate QA sign-off, rollback planning, and deployment execution
- Communicate release status, changes, and impact to all stakeholders
- Conduct post-release reviews and capture lessons learned

### Goals
- Achieve predictable, low-risk releases with minimal rollbacks
- Reduce release-related incidents through rigorous readiness checks
- Maintain clear, timely communication across all parties during release windows

### Typical Interactions
- Works with Developers to validate that all PRs are merged and CI checks pass
- Coordinates with QA/testing to confirm acceptance criteria and smoke tests are complete
- Aligns with the Project Manager on release schedule and stakeholder communication
- Briefs Business Stakeholders and support teams ahead of production deployments
- Partners with the Scrum Master to incorporate release learnings into process improvements
